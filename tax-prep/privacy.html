Information we collect

Through this website. When you submit the consultation form, we receive your name, phone number, email address, the type of tax service you're interested in, your filing status, your preferred meeting method, and any notes you choose to add. We ask you not to send Social Security numbers, ITINs, or bank details through the form.

When you become a client. To prepare your return we collect the information the tax law requires, which may include Social Security numbers or ITINs, income and wage documents, bank account details for direct deposit, and information about your dependents. We collect this only in person or through our secure document portal, never by unencrypted email or the website form.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your consultation request and schedule appointments.
  • To prepare and electronically file your tax returns.
  • To answer IRS or state notices on your behalf when you ask us to.
  • To meet our legal record-keeping obligations.

Federal law (Internal Revenue Code section 7216) restricts how tax preparers use and disclose tax return information. We will not use your tax return information for any purpose other than preparing your return, or disclose it to anyone, unless the law allows it or you give us specific written consent.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only:

  • With the IRS and state tax agencies, to file your returns.
  • With service providers that help us run the business, such as our tax software, e-file transmitter, secure portal, and website form provider (Web3Forms, which forwards form submissions to our email). These providers may use your information only to provide their services to us.
  • When required by law, such as in response to a subpoena.

How we protect it

As a paid tax preparer, we are subject to the Federal Trade Commission's Safeguards Rule and keep a written information security plan. Our safeguards include encrypted document transfer, access limited to staff who need it, multi-factor authentication on our systems, and secure disposal of records we no longer need to keep.
