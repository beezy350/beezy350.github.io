Questions

Frequently asked

How long does it take to get my refund?

The IRS says most e-filed returns with direct deposit are processed within 21 days. Some returns take longer, and timing is set by the IRS, not by us.

Can you guarantee my refund amount?

No. Your refund depends on your income, withholding, and eligibility for credits. We'll show you the figure before filing, but no honest preparer can promise an amount upfront.

Do I need to come in person?

No. You can meet us in person or work with us remotely through our secure upload portal.

What if I get audited?

We'll help you understand the notice and gather documentation. [State whether audit support is included or extra.]

Can you help if I don't have an SSN?

Yes. We can help you prepare a Form W-7 ITIN application with your tax return. [If you are not an IRS Certifying Acceptance Agent, say that original documents or certified copies must be sent to the IRS or reviewed at an IRS Taxpayer Assistance Center.]

Do I file IFTA in every state I drive through?

No. You file one IFTA return each quarter with your base state, reporting miles and fuel for every member state and province. Your base state then sends each state its share of the tax.
